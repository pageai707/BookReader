# BookReader

A personal PDF library on **GitHub Pages**. Organise PDFs in folders, describe them in `meta.toml`, and push. You get a website where you can browse by category, search everything (including the full text) and read in the browser, with nothing to download.

**Features**
- **Categories from your folders:** nested folders become a category tree with a sidebar, breadcrumbs and category pages showing sub-categories, descriptions and counts.
- **Search everything:** titles, authors, tags, categories, years and the full text of every page. A text match opens the PDF at that page with your words highlighted. A word index built ahead of time keeps search fast for large libraries.
- **Tags:** filter any view by one or more tags.
- **Reader:** the full [PDF.js](https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/) viewer (search in document, thumbnails, outline, zoom, print), plus a details panel showing authors, year, publisher, category, tags and source link.
- **Your reading:** "Continue reading" remembers your page in every document, plus favorites and a recently-read list.
- **Browsing:** sort by recently added, title, author, year, pages or size. Grid or list view. Long lists load more as you scroll.
- **Links:** the address always points at the current category, search or page, so you can bookmark or share it.
- Light and dark theme, works on phones, keyboard shortcuts: `/` to search, `Esc` to go back, `i` for document details.
